About WanderLux

Your journey, our passion.

Who We Are

WanderLux is a fictional travel agency developed for an educational website project. The agency concept focuses on personalised holidays, guided tours and customised travel packages for domestic and international destinations.

Our Mission

To make travel planning simple, accessible and enjoyable through clear information, flexible options and customer-focused service.

Our Travel Consultants

Our proposed service model includes travel consultants who help customers choose destinations, compare travel styles and plan itineraries suited to their preferences.

Our Values

Personalisation

Every traveller has different needs and interests.

Transparency

Clear estimates and straightforward travel information.

Customer Care

Friendly communication throughout the planning process.
